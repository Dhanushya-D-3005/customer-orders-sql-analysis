# Customer Orders & Customer Segmentation Analysis

Project Overview

This capstone project focuses on analyzing customer purchasing behavior to identify valuable, loyal, potential, and at-risk customers.

The project uses data-driven customer segmentation to support targeted marketing, personalization, customer retention, and improved customer lifetime value.

Problem Statement

Businesses often struggle to identify and retain valuable customers due to a lack of actionable customer segmentation and purchasing-behavior insights. This can lead to inefficient marketing spend and increased customer churn.

Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Conduct inferential statistical analysis
- Perform RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis
- Segment customers based on purchasing behavior
- Build classification models
- Identify key business insights
- Recommend data-driven customer retention strategies

RFM Analysis

RFM analysis evaluates customers based on:

- **Recency:** How recently a customer made a purchase
- **Frequency:** How often a customer purchases
- **Monetary:** How much a customer spends

The objective is to identify loyal, potential, and at-risk customers and support targeted marketing strategies.
