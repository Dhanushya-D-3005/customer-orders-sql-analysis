# Customer Orders & Customer Segmentation Analysis

## Project Overview

This capstone project focuses on analyzing customer purchasing behavior to identify valuable, loyal, potential, and at-risk customers.

The project uses data-driven customer segmentation to support targeted marketing, personalization, customer retention, and improved customer lifetime value.

## Problem Statement

Businesses often struggle to identify and retain valuable customers due to a lack of actionable customer segmentation and purchasing-behavior insights. This can lead to inefficient marketing efforts and increased customer churn.

## Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Conduct inferential statistical analysis
- Perform RFM analysis
- Segment customers based on purchasing behavior
- Build classification models
- Identify key business insights
- Recommend customer retention strategies

## Methodology

1. Data Understanding
2. Data Preprocessing
3. Exploratory Data Analysis
4. Inferential Statistics
5. RFM Analysis
6. Customer Segmentation
7. Classification Models
8. Business Insights
9. Business Recommendations

## RFM Analysis

RFM analysis evaluates customers using:

- **Recency** – How recently a customer made a purchase
- **Frequency** – How often a customer purchases
- **Monetary** – How much a customer spends

The analysis helps identify valuable, loyal, potential, and at-risk customers.

## Customer Segmentation

Customers are segmented based on their purchasing behavior and RFM scores to support targeted marketing and customer retention strategies.

## Business Impact

The analysis can help businesses:

- Improve customer retention
- Identify high-value customers
- Identify at-risk customers
- Improve marketing personalization
- Optimize marketing efforts
- Increase customer lifetime value
